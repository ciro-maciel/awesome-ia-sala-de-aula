## Education rules

What defines the curriculum, student rights and the teacher's work.

- [BNCC](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaaula-bncc) - The Base Nacional Comum Curricular, in the MEC document. `official` `free`
- [ECA](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaaula-eca) - The Statute of the Child and Adolescent (ECA). `official` `free`
- [LDB](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaaula-ldb) - The Law of Guidelines and Bases of Education (LDB). `official` `free`
- [Lei Brasileira de Inclusão](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaaula-lei-brasileira-de-inclusao) - The Statute of Persons with Disabilities, basis for adapted activity. `official` `free`
- [Lei do piso do magistério](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaaula-lei-do-piso-do-magisterio) - Law 11.738/2008, which establishes the teacher's minimum salary floor. `official` `free`

## Data protection

The law and the authority that apply to client and student data.

- [LGPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaaula-lgpd) - The General Data Protection Law, in the compiled text on the Planalto site. `official` `free`
- [ANPD](https://go.mymarketing.click/iaaula-anpd) - The National Data Protection Authority, with guides and regulations under the LGPD. `official` `free`

## Tags

Each item says what it is. The tags beside it help you choose: official when it comes from the body that sets the standard, open source when the code is published, free or paid based on the usage model, and API and SDK based on how you integrate. Items tagged "by the author" are made by Ciro Cesar Maciel and follow the same rules as all the others.
